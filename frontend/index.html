What Veridoc does — and why it matters

Veridoc is a document intelligence system that answers your questions using only the files you upload — PDFs, Word docs, slide decks, screenshots, or text files. Instead of guessing from general knowledge, Veridoc reads your documents and replies with precision.

Every answer is anchored to its source. You can click any citation and jump straight to the exact page and sentence the answer came from. If the answer isn't in your documents, Veridoc says so — it will never make something up.

This makes Veridoc useful for anyone who deals with information that needs to be accurate and verifiable — students, researchers, and professionals working with contracts, reports, and regulations.
